AppSystem

One platform for call center, invoicing, and team operations — built so teams see only what their role needs, and new modules snap in without touching the core.

Try the live demo View on GitHub

Support and billing, one shared record

Most teams run their call center and their invoicing in separate tools with no shared customer record. AppSystem keeps customers, products, calls, and invoices in one place — while each team only ever sees the modules and data their role grants them.

Multi-tenant from the ground up

Every organization (tenant) gets its own isolated database. A Global Admin oversees all tenants and users from one panel, while each tenant's local admins manage their own teams and roles — without ever seeing another tenant's data.

What's inside

📞

Call Center

Live customer queue with real-time, WebSocket-driven call handling and call logging.

⏰

Alarms & Reminders

Scheduled reminders tied to customers and products, delivered by a background scheduler.

🧾

Invoicing & Companies

Sequential invoice numbering, client/company records, and bank account details.

👥

Customers & Products

A shared customer database and product catalog, with per-customer product status tracking.

🗂️

Teams & Roles

Staff are organized into teams under an account, with per-module roles — one team can run Call Center + Alarms while another only sees Invoices + Companies.

🔐

Secure by default

Email + password login with optional two-factor verification via a one-time magic link.

Built to extend

Every business module — Customers, Invoices, Call Center — is a self-contained "vertical": its own routes, templates, and permissions. New modules are auto-discovered and added to the navigation automatically. No changes to core application code.

  1. 1. Add verticals/<name>/__init__.py with a manifest (label, order, routes)
  2. 2. Add routes and templates for the module
  3. 3. Restart — the module appears in navigation, with roles applied automatically
